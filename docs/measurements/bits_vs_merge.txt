bitmap wins down to steps=0.63; merge wins up to steps=0.39

by row-degree CV: bitmap wins 9/9 of the SKEWED cells (CV>0.5) and 4/5 of the FLAT ones.
The merge's cost is branch mispredicts, and mispredicts come from unpredictable
trip counts -- so the deciding quantity is the VARIANCE of the row degrees, not
the mean of the step count. `_prefer_bits` samples the mean.
Note that `datasets.sparse_rows` gives every row exactly `nnz` non-zeros, so
the whole synthetic sparse grid sits at CV=0 -- degenerate in precisely the
dimension that decides dispatch, and unreachable by real data.
